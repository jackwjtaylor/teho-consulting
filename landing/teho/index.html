Frequently asked

How fast is the briefing?

Typically 2–3 business days. If we need internal data to complete the story, we flag it immediately.

Do you need private data?

Not for the free briefing. Private data only comes into play once you choose to run workshops or sprints.

How fresh is the insight?

We prioritise sources from the last 24 months. Anything older is labelled “Data gap – refresh required”.

Can we white-label the assets?

Yes. Snapshot and reports arrive in Markdown/HTML so you can brand and share them however you like.
