# Pilot Companies (Wave 1)

We are testing the report workflow with three UK firms that have plenty of public data and clear room for AI-led gains.

| Company | HQ | Sector | Why we chose them | Who to contact | Extra notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Gousto | London | Meal-kit delivery | Busy factories, heavy logistics spend, big focus on customer experience. Reviews show love for the recipes but irritation with delivery slip-ups. | Founder/CEO, COO, Chief Digital Officer | Food safety rules apply. Mix of manufacturing, data, and service makes it a rich test case. |
| Bloom & Wild | London | Direct-to-consumer flowers and gifts | Needs sharp forecasting for perishable stock, strong digital brand, expanding into Europe. | CEO, Chief Customer Officer, Head of Operations | Plenty of customer feedback online. Big chance to tailor offers and tidy supply chain planning. |
| Zego | London | Commercial motor insurance | Fast-growing cover for gig-economy drivers with lots of telematics data to manage. | CEO, Chief Product Officer, Chief Risk Officer | Regulated market. AI can help with pricing, claims checks, and compliance reporting. |

## What we do for each pilot

1. Run the `data_inputs_checklist.md` steps to collect the core facts and sources.  
2. Fill in the prompt variables and create both the executive and comprehensive reports.  
3. Review the output by hand, flag issues, and tweak the prompt or data list if needed.  
4. Track outreach results once the reports go out (opens, replies, meetings booked).
