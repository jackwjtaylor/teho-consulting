## 1) Company Snapshot
- **Business:** PHMG (PH Media Group) — global leader in **audio branding + on-hold marketing**, expanding into **caller experience / cloud telephony optimisation**.
- **Core offerings:** On-hold/voicemail/out-of-hours messaging, original music, sonic identity (sonic logos), voiceover + post-production, **Complete Caller Experience** (telephony set-up + caller journey optimisation), analytics/caller consultancy, call routing.
- **Geographic footprint:** UK (Manchester), US (Chicago/Phoenix), Australia (Brisbane), plus Canada & New Zealand.
- **Scale & performance:** ~**800+ employees**; **FY2023 revenue £85.5m** (+14% YoY) with **£16.5m operating profit** (~19% operating margin). Subscription model with ~**91% subscription revenue retained** and **36k+ clients** (FY2023 disclosures).
- **Notable recent moves:**
  - **Product portfolio refresh (Jul 2024):** Brand Symphony (super-premium), Caller Edge (flagship rename), Brand Sound upgrades incl. Sonic Logo + toolkit, add-ons incl. Voiceover Suite + Complete Caller Experience.
  - **Ownership & leadership (2025):** Transitioned to **Employee Ownership Trust (EOT)**; **King’s Award for International Trade**; CEO transition to Jason Daye (Aug 2025).
- **Tech signals:** Salesforce CRM; PHMG Portal (portal.phmg.com); cloud telephony/routing + analytics positioning.

## 2) Top 3 Opportunities
1) **Speed-to-lead automation + geo/segment routing for inbound (web forms, phone, chat)** *(SalesOps & CRM automation | Impact: High | Effort: Medium)*
   - Automate lead creation/dedupe/enrichment and SLA-driven routing in **Salesforce**, with real-time rep alerts and escalation.
   - Expected outcome: higher contact rates and faster first meetings without increasing CAC.

2) **Sales-to-delivery handoff “source of truth” pack in PHMG Portal** *(Client delivery ops | Impact: High | Effort: Medium)*
   - Create a standardized, gated handoff pack (scope, timeline, stakeholders, scripts/pronunciations, routing/telephony requirements) published in **PHMG Portal** and linked to Salesforce.
   - Expected outcome: fewer kickoff delays, fewer clarification loops, faster time-to-first-value.

3) **Creative feedback summarisation + version control for scripts/audio** *(CreativeOps | Impact: High | Effort: Medium)*
   - Summarise customer feedback into actionable changes, manage version diffs/history, route approvals to the right stakeholder, and flag conflicting input/scope creep.
   - Expected outcome: reduced revision loops and approval cycle time, reclaimed production capacity.

## 3) Why Now
- **Commercial momentum + new portfolio complexity:** The 2024 portfolio expansion (Brand Symphony, Caller Edge, add-ons) increases the need for **faster inbound conversion** and **cleaner handoffs** to deliver consistently.
- **Scale demands operational leverage:** With 36k+ clients and rapid new client adds, small inefficiencies in lead handling, handoff, and revision cycles compound into meaningful margin and CX impact.
- **EOT structure heightens focus on durable operating cadence:** Employee ownership typically rewards investments that improve **predictability, throughput, and customer satisfaction**—areas directly targeted by the top opportunities.

## 4) 90-Day Pilot Overview
**Pilot goal:** Improve revenue capture and delivery efficiency across the funnel by implementing two “front-to-back” workflows and validating measurable uplift.

**Scope (recommended):**
- **Workstream A (Weeks 1–6):** *Speed-to-lead + routing*
  - Connect inbound sources (web/chat/call-tracking where available) → Salesforce Lead creation/update.
  - Implement dedupe + enrichment light rules; geo/segment routing; SLA timers; rep alerts.
- **Workstream B (Weeks 3–10):** *Portal handoff pack*
  - Define required fields + gating checklist; auto-generate handoff pack from Salesforce + discovery inputs.
  - Publish in PHMG Portal as the kickoff “source of truth” with role-based permissions.
- **Workstream C (Weeks 6–12):** *Creative feedback summarisation + versioning*
  - Enable structured feedback capture in Portal; summarisation/diff; approval routing and reminders.

**Operating model & guardrails:**
- Human override for merges/routing; full audit logs; privacy-by-region (UK/NA/AU); consent-driven handling for any transcription.

**Pilot design:**
- Select **1–2 regions** (e.g., UK + North America) and **2–3 teams** (Inbound SDR/BDR, Delivery kickoff owners, Creative production).
- Run **A/B comparison** vs baseline for speed-to-lead, kickoff readiness, and revision cycle time.

## 5) KPIs to Track
**Inbound / Revenue KPIs**
- Median **speed-to-lead** (minutes) and **% meeting SLA**
- **Contact rate** within 24 hours
- **Time-to-first-meeting** and inbound **conversion rate**
- **Misroute / reassignment rate**

**Delivery / Handoff KPIs**
- **Kickoff delay rate** due to missing assets/requirements
- **Handoff completeness score** (required fields populated)
- # of **post-handoff clarification loops**
- **Time-to-first-value** (from signature to go-live/first deployed assets)

**CreativeOps KPIs**
- Average **revision count** per project
- **Approval cycle time** (days)
- **Production lead time** and % projects with version conflicts/rework

**Customer outcomes (pilot-level leading indicators)**
- Post-kickoff **CSAT**
- Early-life escalations related to missing scope/requirements

---
*Target uplift (directional, based on opportunity sizing):* 50–70% faster speed-to-lead; 20–40% fewer kickoff delays; 15–30% fewer revision cycles and 20–35% faster approvals.
