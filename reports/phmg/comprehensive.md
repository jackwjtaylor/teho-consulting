## Top Five Opportunity Deep Dives

### 1. Customer Sentiment Analysis

**Problem today:**  
PHMG likely lacks real-time, actionable insights into customer sentiment, limiting its ability to respond to feedback and improve satisfaction.

**AI fix:**  
Deploy Natural Language Processing (NLP) tools to analyse customer communications (emails, social media, surveys) to extract sentiment trends and pain points.

**Delivery needs:**  
- Access to customer interaction data  
- NLP platform integration  
- Data science expertise for model tuning  

**Risks & mitigations:**  
- Data privacy concerns mitigated by anonymisation and compliance checks  
- Model bias addressed through diverse training data  

**ROI narrative:**  
Improved customer retention and upsell opportunities could increase revenue by £200k–£500k annually, with a confidence level of medium due to data gaps.

---

### 2. Process Automation

**Problem today:**  
Manual, repetitive tasks increase operational costs and error rates.

**AI fix:**  
Implement Robotic Process Automation (RPA) to automate routine workflows such as invoicing, data entry, and reporting.

**Delivery needs:**  
- Process mapping and prioritisation  
- RPA software deployment  
- Change management and staff training  

**Risks & mitigations:**  
- Resistance to change mitigated by clear communication and upskilling  
- Process exceptions managed by hybrid human-AI workflows  

**ROI narrative:**  
Potential cost savings of £300k–£700k per year from reduced labour and error correction, with high confidence.

---

### 3. Predictive Sales Analytics

**Problem today:**  
Sales forecasting is imprecise, leading to inventory mismanagement and missed revenue targets.

**AI fix:**  
Use machine learning models to analyse historical sales and external factors for accurate demand prediction.

**Delivery needs:**  
- Historical sales and market data  
- Data engineering and ML model development  
- Integration with sales and inventory systems  

**Risks & mitigations:**  
- Data quality issues addressed by cleansing and validation  
- Model drift managed by continuous retraining  

**ROI narrative:**  
Improved forecasting could boost revenue by £400k–£900k annually, with medium confidence pending data availability.

---

### 4. Personalised Marketing

**Problem today:**  
Generic marketing campaigns yield low engagement and conversion.

**AI fix:**  
Deploy recommendation engines and customer segmentation to deliver personalised offers.

**Delivery needs:**  
- Customer data platform  
- AI recommendation algorithms  
- Marketing automation tools  

**Risks & mitigations:**  
- Privacy compliance ensured by opt-in policies  
- Over-personalisation avoided by balanced content strategies  

**ROI narrative:**  
Increased conversion rates could add £250k–£600k in revenue yearly, with medium-high confidence.

---

### 5. Fraud Detection

**Problem today:**  
Potential exposure to fraudulent transactions or activities.

**AI fix:**  
Implement anomaly detection algorithms to flag suspicious behaviour in real time.

**Delivery needs:**  
- Transactional data access  
- AI model development and tuning  
- Alert and response workflows  

**Risks & mitigations:**  
- False positives minimised by threshold tuning  
- Continuous monitoring to adapt to new fraud patterns  

**ROI narrative:**  
Fraud reduction could save £150k–£400k annually, with medium confidence depending on fraud risk profile.
