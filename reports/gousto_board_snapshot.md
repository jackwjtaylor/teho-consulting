# Gousto AI Opportunity Snapshot
**Date:** 7 Oct 2025  
**Prepared by:** Teho Consulting (Founder: Jack Taylor)  
**Why it matters:** Gousto has strong factories and big menu choice, but late deliveries and refunds are still costing money. The ideas below aim to recover about £8–£12m extra profit within a year.

---

## Key facts _(Confidence: Medium)_
- FY2024 revenue was about **£312m** (up 1%), with **£42m** adjusted operating profit and under **1%** food wasted in the process (Source 4).  
- The Warrington site runs about **140% faster** than older sites and hits **99.97%** picking accuracy thanks to in-house automation (Source 3).  
- Recent Trustpilot comments still mention boxes turning up late, missing items, or food going off too soon (Source 2).

---

## Three AI moves to focus on _(Confidence: Low)_
1. **Courier live view** – bring courier feeds together, predict late drops, and fix problems before customers complain.  
   - Could cut refund credits by about 30%, saving roughly **£5–6m** a year.
2. **Smarter demand planning** – use better forecasting to order the right ingredients for the 200 weekly recipes.  
   - Hitting waste of 0.7% and cutting stock-outs by a fifth could free **£7–9m**.
3. **Support helper** – give the service team an AI assistant that drafts replies and suggests fixes for missing or damaged items.  
   - Faster replies and more self-serve could trim support spend by about **£1.8m**.

---

## What others are doing _(Confidence: Low)_
- HelloFresh UK talks about more personal menus and automation, and rivals like Mindful Chef lean on healthy choice.  
- None of them shout about real-time courier control or AI-backed support yet, so Gousto can claim that ground first.

---

## First 90 days _(Confidence: Low)_
- **Week 2:** Hold a short planning session with Teho to agree goals, data access, and who owns each pilot.  
- **Weeks 3–6:** Trial the support helper with a small team and plug in courier data at Warrington.  
- **Weeks 7–12:** Roll out the forecasting pilot on the top 50 ingredients and share a weekly delivery scorecard with leadership.
