2. **AI Revenue Playbook for Sales Conversion**  
   - *Current problem:* Manual sales follow-ups and stale CRM data limit conversion rates.

3. **Predictive Ops Control Tower**  
   - *Current problem:* Operational delays and quality variance in event fulfilment cause customer dissatisfaction.

     - Exec sceptic rebuttal: “Data quality is poor.” Even partial data integration improves visibility and reduces costly surprises.
