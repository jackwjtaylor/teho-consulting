Top three AI wins

  1. AI Service Desk Triage

    • Current problem: High volume of customer queries with slow response times and backlog (typical in event venues).
    • AI fix: Implement an LLM-powered triage system that drafts responses, routes queries, and escalates complex cases.
    • £ upside: £100k–£250k annual savings from reduced handle time and improved customer retention (Assumption based on UK telco case).
      • Practical example for The Warehouse Project: Automate ticketing support during peak event periods, reducing average handle time by 30%.
      • Exec sceptic rebuttal: “AI can’t handle complex queries.” Evidence shows supervised escalation maintains quality while cutting routine workload.

  2. AI Revenue Playbook for Sales Conversion

    • Current problem: Manual sales follow-ups and stale CRM data limit conversion rates.
    • AI fix: Use AI to generate pipeline briefs, identify at-risk deals, and draft personalised outreach nudges.
    • £ upside: £120k–£300k uplift from a 6–8 percentage point conversion increase (Benchmark from mid-market SaaS).
      • Practical example: Automate follow-ups for event sponsorship and VIP ticket sales, accelerating pipeline velocity.
      • Exec sceptic rebuttal: “Sales teams prefer personal contact.” AI augments rather than replaces, freeing time for high-value calls.

  3. Predictive Ops Control Tower

    • Current problem: Operational delays and quality variance in event fulfilment cause customer dissatisfaction.
    • AI fix: Blend demand forecasts and logistics data to alert teams proactively about risks.
    • £ upside: £30k–£50k annual savings from fewer refunds and improved on-time delivery (Benchmark from grocery e-commerce).
      • Practical example: Real-time alerts for stock or equipment shortages ahead of events.
      • Exec sceptic rebuttal: “Data quality is poor.” Even partial data integration improves visibility and reduces costly surprises.
