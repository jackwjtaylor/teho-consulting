## Top three AI wins  

1. **AI Service Desk Triage for Card Queries**  
   - *Problem:* High volume of manual card-related customer contacts causing delays and backlog (Data gap – exact volumes unknown).  
   - *AI fix:* Deploy LLM-powered triage to draft responses, route intents, and escalate complex cases, reducing average handle time by 20–30%.  
   - *Upside:* £2–3 million annual cost savings from reduced labour and faster resolution (S# telco case study adapted).  
     - Practical example for Lloyds Bank: Integrate AI triage into existing card operations contact centre to automate routine queries and prioritise fraud alerts.  
     - Exec sceptic rebuttal: “AI won’t understand complex card issues.” Evidence shows supervised LLMs escalate edge cases, maintaining quality.  

2. **Predictive Fraud Detection and Risk Scoring**  
   - *Problem:* Manual fraud detection leads to missed cases and false positives, increasing costs and customer friction.  
   - *AI fix:* Use machine learning models to analyse transaction patterns and flag suspicious activity with higher accuracy.  
   - *Upside:* £1.5–2.5 million annual reduction in fraud losses and investigation costs (Industry benchmarks).  
     - Practical example for Lloyds Bank: Deploy AI models on card transaction streams to prioritise alerts and reduce false positives by 15%.  
     - Exec sceptic rebuttal: “AI models are black boxes.” Transparent, explainable AI frameworks ensure compliance and auditability.  

3. **Voice of Customer Insight Engine for Card Service Quality**  
   - *Problem:* Lack of root-cause analysis on card-related complaints slows improvement cycles.  
   - *AI fix:* Cluster and quantify customer feedback to identify top pain points and link them to operational owners.  
   - *Upside:* £1–2 million annual benefit from reduced complaint handling costs and improved retention (S# retail case study).  
     - Practical example for Lloyds Bank: Implement AI to analyse card customer feedback from calls, emails, and surveys, enabling targeted fixes.  
     - Exec sceptic rebuttal: “Feedback volume is too low.” Even moderate volumes yield actionable insights when clustered effectively.
