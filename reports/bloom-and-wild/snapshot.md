## Executive Summary (Confidence: High)  
Bloom & Wild Ltd is a leading UK-based ecommerce flower and gifting company with £82m revenue in FY2024, growing rapidly via innovation and European expansion (S4, S2, S3). Their core strengths lie in letterbox flower delivery, subscriptions, and curated gifts, supported by data-driven marketing and supply chain operations. However, challenges remain in forecasting demand, personalizing customer engagement, and optimizing logistics under strict regulations.  

Top AI opportunities include:  
1. **Demand Forecasting AI** to reduce waste and improve inventory (high impact, moderate effort).  
2. **Personalized Gift Recommendation Engine** leveraging customer data to boost conversion and AOV.  
3. **AI-Powered Customer Support Chatbot** to reduce costs and improve response times.  

Teho Consulting can help Bloom & Wild rapidly prototype these AI solutions, integrate them with existing systems like Braze and Looker, and ensure GDPR-compliant data use. This will drive growth, reduce costs, and mitigate risks in a competitive market.
