## Three AI plays to tackle now _(Confidence: Low)_
1. **Seasonal Demand Brain** – Probabilistic forecasts + scenario planning to match stock to seasonal peaks.  
   - _Impact:_ Waste down 2–3%, availability up 1–2% → ~£5–£6m margin.  
2. **Courier Pulse** – Multi-carrier telemetry, ETA predictions, proactive customer comms.  
   - _Impact:_ 25% refund reduction → ~£3–£4m preserved (needs SLA data).  
3. **Customer Lifetime Lens** – CLV & churn models feeding Braze journeys across Bloom & Wild, Bloomon, Bergamotte.  
   - _Impact:_ +5% repeat orders → ~£6m revenue.

Supporting moves: carbon data tracker for ESG proof, creative automation to scale “Care Wildly” storytelling, subscription tuner, and M&A data harmoniser.
