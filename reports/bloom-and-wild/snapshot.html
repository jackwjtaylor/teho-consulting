Bloom & Wild AI Opportunity Snapshot

Date: 17 Oct 2025
Prepared by: Teho Consulting (Jack Taylor)
Why it matters: Bloom & Wild is scaling across Europe with acquisitions and sustainability commitments, but seasonal demand swings and courier visibility gaps risk margin and loyalty. These AI moves aim to unlock £12–£16m within 12 months.

Key facts (Confidence: Medium)

  • £75m Series D + £50m extension funding to power European expansion, data science and sustainability programmes (Sources S1, S3).
  • 160% revenue growth in 2020 with first profitable year; targeting >£200m revenue post Bloomon/Bergamotte acquisitions (Sources S1, S3).
  • Operates in 8 European markets with letterbox packaging, Sainsbury’s retail tie-up and carbon-neutral commitments (Sources S1, S2, S3).

Three AI plays to tackle now (Confidence: Low)

  1. Seasonal Demand Brain – Probabilistic forecasts + scenario planning to match stock to seasonal peaks.
    • Impact: Waste down 2–3%, availability up 1–2% → ~£5–£6m margin.
  2. Courier Pulse – Multi-carrier telemetry, ETA predictions, proactive customer comms.
    • Impact: 25% refund reduction → ~£3–£4m preserved (needs SLA data).
  3. Customer Lifetime Lens – CLV & churn models feeding Braze journeys across Bloom & Wild, Bloomon, Bergamotte.
    • Impact: +5% repeat orders → ~£6m revenue.

Supporting moves: carbon data tracker for ESG proof, creative automation to scale “Care Wildly” storytelling, subscription tuner, and M&A data harmoniser.

90-day plan (Confidence: Low)

  • Week 2: AI readiness workshop with Teho—align KPIs, assess data landscape, agree pilot squad owners.
  • Weeks 3–6: Demand data audit + MVP forecasting model for Mother’s Day UK; secure courier data-sharing agreements.
  • Weeks 6–12: Launch CLV segmentation test in Braze; deploy courier pulse pilot in UK; draft carbon data blueprint and sustainability dashboard.

What’s missing

  • Courier partner list and refund metrics (requires ops team).
  • FY2024 financials and updated sustainability figures.
  • Verified outreach email for CEO/press. Capture before outreach send.
